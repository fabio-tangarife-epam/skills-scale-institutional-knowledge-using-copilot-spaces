# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. Depending on project size and team structure, a persona may be assigned to a dedicated person or shared among team members. Assigning a persona clarifies accountability; it does not replace the responsibilities of existing roles.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Test Lead

### Role Summary
The QA/Test Lead coordinates the quality strategy and validation activities so that delivery meets agreed acceptance criteria and quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define a proportionate test approach, including automation and exploratory testing
- Coordinate test coverage, defect triage, and quality-readiness reporting
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigations
- Confirm that test evidence and acceptance results are available for release decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to build testability, maintain automated tests, and resolve defects
- Works with the Product Manager to clarify acceptance criteria and communicate feature acceptance or gaps
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on test dependencies, milestones, blockers, and readiness reporting

---

## UX/UI Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
The UX/UI Designer or Researcher helps ensure that product decisions and experiences address user needs and are usable and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Create and validate user flows, interaction designs, and prototypes
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations and share design rationale
- Gather feedback and recommend experience improvements

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with the Product Manager to connect user needs, research insights, and product outcomes
- Works with Developers to assess feasibility and support faithful, accessible implementation
- Aligns with the Project Manager on research and design activities, dependencies, and review milestones

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead/Architect guides technical direction and helps the team make maintainable design and integration decisions aligned with project needs.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate technical design and document significant decisions
- Guide architecture, interfaces, and integration approaches
- Identify technical risks and dependencies and propose mitigations
- Support code quality through technical guidance and reviews

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers on implementation choices, reviews, and resolving technical blockers
- Works with the Product Manager to explain technical trade-offs and their impact on product scope or outcomes
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on dependencies, estimates, technical risks, and delivery implications

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
The DevOps/Release Engineer enables reliable build, deployment, and operation of the product through delivery automation and operational readiness practices.

### Responsibilities
- Develop or coordinate CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Support environment, configuration, and release readiness
- Establish or maintain monitoring and operational verification practices
- Document deployment, rollback, and recovery procedures

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to automate builds and deployments and improve observability
- Coordinates with the QA/Test Lead on environments and release verification
- Partners with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, risks, and stakeholder communication
- Aligns with the Product Manager on release scope and customer-impact considerations

---

## Security or Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
The Security or Privacy Lead advises the team on security and privacy requirements, risk management, and appropriate assurance activities.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements and review points
- Facilitate risk assessments and recommend proportionate mitigations
- Advise on secure design, data handling, and privacy considerations
- Coordinate security or privacy reviews and communicate unresolved risks

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers and the Technical Lead/Architect to incorporate secure design and implementation practices
- Works with the Product Manager to clarify requirements and explain risk-related trade-offs
- Coordinates with the Project Manager to track risks, review dependencies, and escalate issues through the appropriate process
